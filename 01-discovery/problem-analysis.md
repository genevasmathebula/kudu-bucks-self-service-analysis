# Problem Analysis: Kudu Bucks Self-Service Top-Up Solution

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this analysis is to understand the challenges students may experience when topping up their Kudu Bucks balance for library printing and identify opportunities to improve the process.

This analysis is based on my personal experience as a Wits student. It is an independent Business Analysis portfolio project and is not an official Wits initiative.

## 2. Problem Statement

The current cash-based top-up process can be inconvenient when cash-loading machines reject banknotes or are unavailable. Students may need to retry the transaction, travel to another machine elsewhere on campus, wait until later, or rely on another student to pay for their printing.

These difficulties can consume time and create uncertainty when students need to print academic materials. A digital self-service top-up option could provide an alternative way to add funds without depending on a functioning cash-loading machine.

## 3. Current Challenges

### 3.1 Cash Rejection

Based on personal experience, machines sometimes reject banknotes. Repeated attempts may be necessary, and the reason for rejection is not confirmed.

### 3.2 Unpredictable Machine Availability

A machine may work on one occasion but be unavailable or fail to accept cash on another.

### 3.3 Distance Between Machines

The machines are located in different areas of campus. Finding another available machine may require walking from East Campus to West Campus or travelling between other locations.

### 3.4 Multiple Failed Attempts

Several machines may be unavailable or unsuitable for completing a top-up, leaving the student without a successful alternative.

### 3.5 Reliance on Other Students

When unable to top up, a student may ask another student to use their Kudu Bucks balance to pay for printing and then reimburse them.

### 3.6 Delayed or Abandoned Printing

If no practical top-up option is available, the student may postpone printing or give up trying at that time.

## 4. Current Process

Based on personal experience, the process can be described as follows:

1. The student needs to print documents and requires sufficient Kudu Bucks.
2. The student goes to a cash-loading machine.
3. The student inserts cash.
4. If the machine accepts the cash and completes the top-up, the student can proceed with printing.
5. If the machine rejects the cash or cannot be used, the student may retry with the same or different notes.
6. If the problem continues, the student may look for another machine, try again later, ask another student for assistance, or abandon the attempt.

This is a preliminary process description based on personal experience. The exact steps and system behaviour have not been independently verified.

## 5. Proposed Improvement Opportunity

Explore a mobile-friendly self-service solution that would allow students to:

- Access a simulated Kudu Bucks balance.
- Select a top-up amount.
- Initiate an electronic payment through an authorised payment method.
- Review and confirm the transaction.
- Receive confirmation and view the updated balance after successful processing.

The proposed solution is conceptual. Its feasibility would depend on access to the relevant system, an authorised payment provider, security requirements and approval from the appropriate stakeholders.

## 6. Expected Benefits

Potential benefits to investigate include:

- Reduced dependence on physical cash-loading machines.
- Less time spent travelling between machines.
- Greater convenience when topping up.
- Fewer situations where students need to rely on other students to pay for printing.
- A more accessible alternative when cash-loading machines are unavailable.

These are expected benefits, not measured outcomes. They would need to be validated through further analysis and testing.

## 7. Assumptions and Limitations

- The analysis is based on personal observations rather than formal research involving a representative sample of students.
- The current technical reasons for cash rejection and machine unavailability are unknown.
- The availability of an existing digital top-up service has not been fully verified.
- Access to internal Wits systems, operational data and technical documentation has not been established.
- No real payments, financial transactions or integrations will be implemented as part of this portfolio project.
- Any sample balances, transactions or payment confirmations used in the prototype will be simulated.
