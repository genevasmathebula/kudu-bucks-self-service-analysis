# Future-State Process Analysis

## 1. Purpose

This document describes a proposed improved process for topping up Kudu Bucks digitally.

The objective is to reduce students' reliance on physical cash-loading machines when funding their printing balances.

## 2. Proposed Process

1. The student identifies a need to top up their Kudu Bucks balance.
2. The student accesses the proposed digital top-up service.
3. The system authenticates the student and identifies the correct Kudu Bucks account.
4. The student enters the desired top-up amount.
5. The system displays the amount and relevant transaction details for review.
6. The student initiates payment using a supported digital payment method.
7. The system determines the confirmed payment outcome.
8. If the payment succeeds, the top-up is applied to the student's Kudu Bucks balance and confirmation is displayed.
9. The student proceeds to print.
10. If the payment fails, the system displays an appropriate message and provides a safe next step.
11. If the payment outcome is unknown, the system communicates that the transaction is still being checked and avoids encouraging a duplicate payment.

## 3. Expected Benefits

- Reduced dependence on physical cash-loading machines.
- Greater convenience when students need to fund their printing balances.
- Clearer feedback about top-up outcomes.
- Potentially less time spent looking for another working cash-loading machine.

These benefits are expected outcomes and have not yet been measured.

## 4. Dependencies and Risks

- Integration with the existing Kudu Bucks system must be investigated.
- A suitable digital payment method must be identified.
- Student authentication and account identification must be designed securely.
- Payment confirmation and balance updates must be reliable.
- Duplicate payments, failed transactions and delayed confirmations must be handled appropriately.
- Relevant university and system stakeholders would need to approve the proposed approach.

## 5. Assumptions and Limitations

- The process is a proposed concept, not a description of an existing Wits service.
- The feasibility of integrating digital payments with Kudu Bucks has not been confirmed.
- No real payments or student data are used in the portfolio demonstration.
- The process should be validated with potential users and relevant stakeholders.
