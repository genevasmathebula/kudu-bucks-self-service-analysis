
# Stakeholder Analysis: Kudu Bucks Self-Service Top-Up

## 1. Purpose

This stakeholder analysis identifies the individuals and groups who may be affected by, contribute to, or influence the proposed Kudu Bucks self-service top-up solution.

The stakeholders listed below are proposed based on the conceptual project. Their actual roles, interests and responsibilities would need to be validated through stakeholder engagement.

## 2. Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Interest in the Solution | Potential Influence | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wits students | Convenient and reliable access to Kudu Bucks funds for printing. | Medium | Gather feedback on current challenges and usability needs. |
| University IT team | Secure, reliable integration with existing university systems. | High | Discuss technical feasibility, integration and support requirements. |
| Kudu Bucks system owner or administrator | Accurate account balances and successful transaction processing. | High | Validate system capabilities, business rules and balance update processes. |
| Finance or payments team | Accurate payment records, reconciliation and handling of failed transactions. | High | Confirm payment processes, reconciliation requirements and exception handling. |
| Library staff | Fewer printing disruptions and less need to assist students with cash-loading problems. | Medium | Understand the impact of current top-up difficulties on printing services. |
| Digital payment provider | Secure processing and reliable payment status information. | Medium to High | Confirm supported payment methods, transaction status handling and integration requirements. |

## 3. Key Stakeholder Needs

### Students
- Access a convenient top-up method.
- Know whether a payment has succeeded.
- Avoid duplicate payments.
- Have their Kudu Bucks balance updated accurately.

### University IT and System Administrators
- Protect student accounts and transaction information.
- Ensure reliable integration with existing systems.
- Maintain accurate account balances.
- Support troubleshooting and system maintenance.

### Finance or Payments Team
- Reconcile payments with top-up transactions.
- Identify and resolve failed or disputed payments.
- Maintain accurate transaction records.

### Library Staff
- Reduce disruption caused by unavailable cash-loading machines.
- Help students understand alternative top-up options.

## 4. Assumptions and Limitations

- The stakeholder list is proposed and has not been validated through interviews or workshops.
- The actual owner and administrator of the Kudu Bucks system have not been confirmed.
- The responsibilities of university IT, finance and any payment provider would need to be verified.
- No stakeholder consultation has been conducted as part of this portfolio exercise.
