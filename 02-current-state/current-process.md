# Current-State Process Analysis

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the current process a student follows when attempting to top up their Kudu Bucks balance to pay for printing at Wits.

The purpose is to understand the existing process, identify challenges and establish opportunities for improvement before proposing a self-service top-up solution.

## 2. Current Process Overview

Based on personal experience, the current process generally involves:

1. The student identifies a need to top up their Kudu Bucks balance.
2. The student goes to a cash-loading machine.
3. The student taps their student card on the machine.
4. The student selects the load or top-up option.
5. The student inserts cash into the machine.
6. The student checks whether the top-up is successful.
7. If successful, the student proceeds with printing.
8. If unsuccessful, the student may retry with the same or different notes, look for another machine, ask another student to print using their Kudu Bucks, or abandon the attempt for the time being.

## 3. Identified Challenges

The following challenges have been identified from personal experience:

- Cash-loading machines may reject cash.
- Retrying a transaction does not always resolve the problem.
- Students may need to look for another machine, potentially travelling between campus areas.
- Multiple machines may be unavailable at the same time.
- Students may need to rely on another student to print on their behalf.
- Students may postpone or abandon printing when they cannot top up successfully.

These observations have not yet been independently verified across the wider student population.

## 4. Business Opportunity

A self-service digital top-up solution could potentially allow students to transfer funds from a bank account to their Kudu Bucks balance using a mobile device.

The proposed solution aims to reduce reliance on physical cash-loading machines and provide students with a more convenient way to fund printing transactions.

The feasibility of integrating with the existing Kudu Bucks system, payment methods, security requirements and institutional processes would need to be investigated.

## 5. Assumptions and Limitations

- This analysis is based primarily on personal experience and has not yet been validated through interviews or surveys.
- The existing technical architecture and payment integration options have not been confirmed.
- The proposed solution is an independent portfolio concept and is not an official Wits project.
- No real student information, financial transactions or institutional system integrations are used in this project.
